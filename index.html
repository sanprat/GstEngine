About Tiny GST Engine

Tiny GST Engine is an Apache 2.0 open-source edge AI parser for Indian GST invoices compliant with CGST Section 31. It extracts supplier GSTINs, validates Mod-36 checksums, extracts line items, reconciles taxes, and produces TallyPrime Purchase Vouchers without sending financial data to third-party APIs.
